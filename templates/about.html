About Guidance.AI

A centralized, AI-powered project management and evaluation platform designed exclusively to modernize academic institutions.

🎯 Our Mission

Traditional academic project workflows are often plagued by inefficiencies, from duplicate idea submissions to fragmented communication. Guidance.AI was built to bridge this gap. By integrating intelligent AI validation, structured collaboration, and centralized tracking, we empower students to focus on innovation while providing faculty with the tools they need for seamless evaluation and oversight.

🛡️ AI-Powered Validation

Say goodbye to redundant projects. Our system utilizes advanced embedding technologies to detect plagiarism and flag duplicate idea submissions instantly, ensuring academic integrity.

👥 Smart Team Management

Eliminate the chaos of group formation. Guidance.AI enforces strict team constraints and provides a centralized hub to facilitate efficient, organized collaboration among students.

✅ Structured Approvals

We replace messy email threads with a clean, transparent faculty approval workflow. Proposals are reviewed, tracked, and automatically converted into active projects upon approval.
